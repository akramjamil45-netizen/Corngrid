Privacy

Portfolio visibility

Choose which linked trading accounts appear on your public portfolio. Only selected accounts and their portfolio data are shown publicly. You can keep everything private or revoke public access at any time.

Public data shown

For each selected account, public pages show its masked account identifier, account summary, open positions and recent trade details. They never show your email, broker server, full account number, ticket or deal identifiers, or trading credentials. Accounts you do not select remain private.

Data collected

When you link an EA, CornGrid can store the MT5 account identifier, broker/server, account currency, balance and equity snapshots, open positions, imported trade deals, EA version, and heartbeat timestamps sent by that installation.

How data is used

This information is used to display account monitoring and portfolio analytics to the authenticated owner of the linked account. No MT5 trading password is requested or stored.

Retention and export

Linked account data remains stored until the account is unlinked or the applicable account-retention process removes it. Contact support for a personal data export or deletion request.
